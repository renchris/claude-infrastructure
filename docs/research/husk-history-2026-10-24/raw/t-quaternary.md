## Why it keeps coming back (evidence-backed)

1. **Many single-cause fixes, and fail-closed gates that still conflict.** ROOT-CAUSE.md:43-46 says "each actor's predicate is true and the union strands the session". husk-panes-2026-10-22/REPORT.md lists "every close is one attempt" and "no single final writer". Each fix (d150099dd, 307655279, 996805060, 948810da6, 99acf1d9f) adds or narrows one actor. None makes the close and verdict path agree on a single ending.
2. **The work-at-risk check is cwd-wide, not the session's own paths.** It is `git status --porcelain | grep -c .` (`bin/cc-pane-close:510`, unchanged since 2026-10-01). Shared checkouts are always dirty: 112 changes on 10-21 and 138 on 10-25, measured in transcripts. So no session there can ever pass gate 4. The session's own owned-path proof and its `--allow-dirty` decision are not carried to the post-exit closer. The verdict then turns that refusal into ⛔ RESUME (`cc-pane-verdict:86-87`).
3. **Verdict painters fall back to the alarming verdict when intent is unproven.** This happened on 10-10 (a lead-ordered teardown painted ⛔ NOT A CLEAN EXIT, 23 of 28 fleet-wide) and again on 10-24 ("Good to close: yes" painted ⛔ resume). The 10-10 fix (307655279) was scoped to teammates. The 10-21 intent stamp (948810da6) passes gate 1, but gate 4 still overrides it.
4. **The fullscreen guard makes husks by design.** Since 996805060 (10-16), self-close of the last pane in a fullscreen window exits 70 and leaves the shell for ⌘W. The 10-16 brief declared "the husks are gone". Any fired session in such a window will husk, and the downstream gates then have to classify it correctly.
5. **Self-close ends the transcript before its own result is recorded.** 7555024a, 5674517a and dccaf095 all end on the self-close tool_use with no tool_result. So the session never sees rc 70 and cannot fall back. Neither the operator nor later research finds the failure in the transcript; it lives only in logs.
6. **"Done" is claimed from one field proof, then the problem recurs.** Examples: pane 695 (09-09) and pane 168 (10-10, b68fa8a25), followed by new husks on 10-20, 10-22 and 10-24. The ORIGIN LEAD re-briefs (10-11, 10-13) carry the claim "182 landed the husk fix" forward as settled.
