## 3. Why it keeps coming back (evidence-backed)

1. **The fix lands in the consumer that failed, not in the shared predicate.** Examples:
   - Per-file dirt attribution (`78f89d73`, 08-03) reached teammate-auto-shutdown and the ledger hooks. The post-exit closer's gate 4 (`bin/cc-pane-close:511`) still asks the whole cwd.
   - The keep-work bgwork answer was fixed for the limit-transplant lane (`22df298c6`, 10-10) and recurred in the self-recycle lane (10-14, `b0ace087f`).
   - There were six composer-parser copies (recycle-failures REPORT:25) and four registration readers broken by one format change (`b6bf10c0c` → round2 RC2).
2. **Several classifiers judge "is this pane done?" and disagree. The session's own verdict is not one of their inputs.**
   - On 10-24: cc-husk-sweep said COLLECTED, cc-pane-close refused at gate 4, and cc-pane-verdict said RESUME.
   - The session's `--allow-dirty` reaches none of cc-pane-close, cc-pane-verdict, cc-husk-sweep, the watchdog or pane-close-retry (`rg allow-dirty`: 0 hits; measured).
   - The CONTRACT said DONE includes "Good to close: yes". The implementation maps a refusal string to RESUME first (`cc-pane-verdict:86`).
3. **The husk is the designed fallback for every refusal.**
   - cc-pane-runner closes only on rc 0 or 75 (husk-mass-sigterm:71-73). "The open pane is intended" (:10).
   - Fail-closed guards refuse whenever kitty is deaf, a dialog is up, or the window is fullscreen-last. Each new refusal reason therefore creates a new husk class rather than a failure.
   - F6 (`c148eeca5`) and the it2-kitty dialog guard (RECYCLE_KITTY_FIXES:74) added kept-shell paths on purpose.
4. **The window layout plus the stock-kitty decision structurally block automatic close of the last pane.** Every OS window is native fullscreen. Decision 891ea9e9d0f3 keeps stock kitty, and upstream declined the deferred close. The guard can therefore never auto-close a sole pane: kitty-lastpane-move README:96-104, round2 RC3, and 7555024a's rc70.
5. **The actuator channel (kitty remote control) fails under the fleet's normal load.** Deaf or stuck kitty: 10-01 (socket queue full), 10-09 (F1, ~45/core), 10-22 (3 h EOF, load 200-450, upstream #10632 unfixed in any release). Background-QoS daemons starve at that load (round2 RC5). Every close is either one-shot or depends on that channel.
6. **Verification happens in a topology that hides the failure.**
   - Waves A/B were checked only from inside kitty, and the stubs answered `session list` regardless of env (round2 RC7).
   - The lr-stranded C7 defect surfaced only in the field proof.
   - No fixture covers "shared cwd with sibling dirt + Good to close: yes".
7. **Investigations are incident-scoped, and plans close on "landed", not on outcome.** Each round freezes scope to its panes and marks the plan `status: complete`. TEAMMATE_SELFCLOSE's reopen found "the outcome metric had never moved, and nobody was reading it". Pane-end-of-life's C6 clean-up has no recorded read-back. The close-behavior census is still pending (10-26).
8. **Upstream surfaces drift under screen-scraping actuators.** Claude Code changes: the slash menu (`f14b42653` regression), bgwork dialog options, non-interactive mode on piped stdout (10-14). kitty: #10632, fsfix. Each change re-breaks typed or read-back paths.
9. **Kill-class husks keep coming from structural exposure that was left unfixed.** Briefs travel in argv, so pattern kills hit sibling sessions (4 incidents; the structural fix was not taken, husk-mass-sigterm:96-100). An unattributed SIGTERM is still open (a7-sigterm.md).
