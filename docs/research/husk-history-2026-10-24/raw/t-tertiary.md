## Why it keeps coming back (evidence-backed)

1. **Each fix adds a fail-safe refusal, and every refusal ends in a kept pane.** The refusals so far: a transplant-tombstone class (09-20 c26162f3; 09-22 pane 500), "a different window" (10-01 pane 76), the whole-cwd dirt gate (d150099dd, 10-01) and the fullscreen last-pane guard (62372906a / 265790f36 / 1211646fb, 10-15). Each is right locally ("the gate is right to refuse", c26162f3). Each still makes a new husk class, because nothing owns the pane after a refusal. On 10-24, rc70 and then gate 4 refused the pane 21 or more times.
2. **Several verdict writers use different predicates, and each fix patches one of them.** The writers are the crash attributor (c53ce8bb7), lead-crash-watchdog (e44823f02, 2c94cf6d8, 7f7b9c293), cc-husk-sweep (bbc40d207, 904fdd205), cc-pane-close (d150099dd, 948810da6, 54acfdb98) and cc-pane-verdict (99acf1d9f, 03696fdd5). COLLECTED was taught to cc-husk-sweep and the watchdog on 10-01. cc-pane-verdict was born on 10-22 deriving RESUME from the closer's refusal text. Result on 10-24: the sweep said COLLECTED (close the pane) and the banner said "⛔ resume".
3. **"Work at risk" is measured as whole-cwd git dirt, not the session's own edits.** Shared roots (~/Development/personal with 138 sibling files; agent-context-sync `main` on 10-22) always hold someone else's dirt, so "Good to close: yes" plus a self-close stamp still loses to gate 4. Sources: cc-pane-close:509-511, cc-husk-sweep:508-517, investigators.md:36,47.
4. **The default under uncertainty is RESUME or keep.** The 09-08 page prescribed `cc-husk-sweep --resume`, the 09-20 watchdog page did too, the 10-18 h-file defect 2 was "dirty RESUME", and on 10-24 the verdict mapped "uncommitted" to RESUME. Each time the "finished" evidence is a special case added afterwards (RETIREMENT, COLLECTED, self-close stamp), and a later gate can still override it.
5. **Fixes are scoped per incident lane, so no single end-of-life contract is ever enforced.** The briefs each freeze one lane: in-place LR (59681f3c / b814f44e), context death (7c8201f8), recycle-dead custody (21fc48d5), teammate close F-a..F-e (d86e6bd4), seven lifecycle fixes (c197cdd9), lr-wake waves B/C/E/F (5a6b8a40), pane-eol-c (dfb193b5). Meanwhile the operator restated the same contract at least four times: 09-22 (7c8201f8), 09-28 (21fc48d5), 10-01 (d86e6bd4) and 10-18 (5a6b8a40 ask (c)).
6. **Actuation depends on kitty, and kitty is unreliable** (remote-control socket refusing, a wedged kitty that does not reap, the fullscreen last pane). Even a correct verdict cannot close the pane. Sources: husk-panes-2026-09-30.md:77; d86e6bd4 at 17:48Z ("kitty is wedged"); 10-24 rc70.
7. **Fixes are proven on fixtures and lands, not on a live pane closing, and some never land or need re-landing.** Examples: pane-eol-c lacked 9 of 9 paths on trunk (dfb193b5, 10-23); Wave F was deployed on the degraded tier with post-deploy checks "cut short by the saturated bats gate" (b43a2afc final text); in d86e6bd4 the auto-mode classifier blocked closing the live husks, so the operator had to run the step.
