Memory is not the binding constraint: **no**. In the only multi-session batch on record, all 8 kernel-safety reads (1 batch + 7 workers, terms `headroom,segments`) admitted (MEASURED-S: `jq 'select(.caller|test("^lr-move"))' ~/.claude/autonomy/idl.jsonl` gives 8 admit, 0 refuse), box headroom stayed 30.96-34.94 GB against a 4 GB floor and compressor segments 4.1-4.6% against a 90% ceiling from 06:00Z to 06:19Z (MEASURED-S: `~/.claude/logs/capacity-alarm.jsonl`, 61 s sampler, 17 samples; 33.42-34.94 GB inside the batch's own 06:07-06:14Z), while 3 of 7 workers waited 166.8, 167.2 and 167.3 s for one of 4 slots and the first of them took its slot 0.455 s after wave 1's first proof released one (MEASURED-S: `jq` over the 7 `<sid>.events.jsonl`; slot wait 503.7 s of 1,751.3 worker-seconds = 29%; mean slot hold 169.7 s, n=7). The bound is a literal: move width 4 (READ-S `lr-move-worker.sh:50`, `lr-move-lib.sh:179` "default 4 until a canary measures a safe width"), recover pool 2 and `--one` lane 2 (READ-S `lr-fleet.sh:1362`, `:1381`); none takes a memory input. On the recover lane 38 of 480 `fleet/one-*` runs logged the cap-of-2 wait (MEASURED-S `grep -l`), against 6 runs with any capacity wait (9 lines: 8 active-ceiling, 1 segments at 54.27% under the old 50% ceiling; MEASURED-S `grep -h`). ## Ranked changes

**Smallest change that removes the bound.** No code change is needed to lift the move-lane width: `cc-lr move --slots W` already writes `plan.json .slots` (READ-S `bin/cc-lr:1246,1313-1316`) and the worker reads that first (READ-S `lr-move-worker.sh:50`). Every listed refusal stays where it is, because none of them lives in the slot (q6 §4: census, `cl_resolve`, lr-handoff precheck, per-sid claim), and actuation stays with the poller (request file, `lrp_move_kick`, detached runner). What is not established is that W above 4 is safe or faster on this box: nothing has run above 4, and the one batch at 4 raised 2 `HANDOFF-RECYCLE-BOOT-INDETERMINATE` alarms in 7 moves (MEASURED-S: `jq` on the two alarm files stamped 06:12:38Z in `~/.claude/handoff-alarms/`, panes 15 and 16). So the responsible minimum is rows 1, 4, 6, 7, 8 below, then a ramp on the next real batch (row 15). With the kick fix and no slot wait, the 7-session batch is about 270-345 s instead of 523 s (EST-S: hop 5-80 s for the rest of an in-flight tick, tick run p50 36 s + 3 s + longest session without its queue 218 s + 44 s close; assumes per-session time does not grow with width, which the record cannot show).

**What clears 90% after cross-check:** rows 1-3 (above 90, each on a receipt I opened) and rows 4-5 (at 90). **What does not, and why:** every widening (rows 15, 16, 19, 20) because n=1 batch, nothing measured above width 4 or above 6 first turns per account, and the same batch shows load1 150 and 2 boot alarms; every wait-shortening (row 17) because about half of the worker-seconds sit in gaps the records cannot place until row 6 lands; the surface and spread work (rows 9-11, 18) because they are designs with no run behind them and one of them rests on an unreproduced defect.
