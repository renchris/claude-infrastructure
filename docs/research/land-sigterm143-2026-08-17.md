# The SIGTERM-143 land bleed — what was fixed, what was refuted, what is still open

**Status 2026-08-17: PARTIALLY fixed. The bleed is REDUCED, not stopped.** Two fixes landed and one
is live; two further hypotheses were measured and REFUTED. The residual cause is not yet named.

This file exists because the refuted half is the expensive half. Everything below was measured on
this box; nothing is inferred from a plausible story.

## The symptom

`scripts/ship-land.sh` reported `verdict=killed signal=SIGTERM` on land after land — 543
`refs/land/failed/*` pins since 2026-08-10, every blocked re-land row reading `ship-land exited 143
(SIGTERM)`, across 6 branches for 5 days.

## CONFIRMED cause 1 — cc-reaper's garbage arm classified the land path (fixed, live)

`orphan-bash` is any launchd-parented bash past 600 s whose argv misses the whitelist, and neither
`ship-land.sh` nor `desk-land.sh` was on it. A backgrounded `handoff-fire.sh land …` — the
non-blocking path `desk-land.sh`'s own header offers — is exactly that shape.

Evidence: of 118 killed lands inside the reaper-log window, 97 (82%) died 2-10 s BEFORE a
`garbage: N TERMed` line, median 3 s, which is that arm's own TERM→KILL `sleep 3`. The 15 s
post-sweep shadows cover 2.10% of the window, so chance predicts ~2 of 118; observed 97 is a **39x
enrichment**. Reproduced deterministically from the live process table with whitelisted
`postland-verify` surviving in the same fixture as the control.

Fixed in `a87f32c66` (whitelist + a kill-time argv re-check, since the old re-check was `ucomm ~
^bash$`, vacuous for the three classes that dominate the candidate set). **Verified live**, and
verified effective in the narrow sense: since it went live, `grep 'garbage: TERM'` over the reaper
log shows **not one ship-land/desk-land classified** — the arm reaps `mailbox-wake-arm.sh` (114),
`autonomy-sweep.sh` (16), bats wrappers (12) and assorted tools, and no land.

## CONFIRMED cause 2 — the whitelist bound one orphan class out of four (fixed, landed)

`wl` was consulted by the `orphan-bash` branch ALONE. `timeout`/`gtimeout` sit on the `orphan-tool`
list at a **120 s** floor, and wrapping a command in a bound is how a whitelisted daemon is normally
invoked — so `timeout -k 10 900 bash …/postland-verify.sh`, reparented to launchd, was reaped while
the identical script invoked WITHOUT a bound was protected at 600 s. Protection depended on whether
somebody had put a `timeout` in front of it. Fixed in `c92c6fe77`.

**This is a real defect and worth fixing on its own, but it does NOT explain the land deaths** — see
the refutation below. Do not treat it as the answer.

## The measurement that matters — and the denominator that nearly hid it

After cause 1 went live at 2026-08-16T23:02Z, kills CONTINUED. Measured over the following 7 h,
against the 12 h before:

| population | PRE | POST |
|---|---|---|
| all land.log rows | 14/98 = 14.3% | 14/182 = 7.7% |
| `total_s >= 10s` | 14/74 = 18.9% | 12/81 = 14.8% |
| `total_s >= 60s` | 12/58 = **20.7%** | 11/69 = **15.9%** |

🚨 **The all-rows row is POLLUTED and must not be quoted.** Survived rows have a median `total_s` of
**4 s** — most entries are instant no-ops (refusals, nothing-to-land, precheck exits), not land
attempts. Quoting 7.7% "vs" 14.3% compares different population mixes and overstates the
improvement. The `>=60s` row is the honest one: **20.7% → 15.9%**, a ~23% relative reduction. And 2
of those 11 post kills are the investigator's own `gtimeout 2400` bound, not an external killer.

**The bleed is reduced, not stopped.**

## REFUTED — do not re-derive these

1. **"`timeout` reaping has a process-group blast radius."** `timeout` is spawned without
   `--foreground`, and `postland-verify.sh:92` notes that this gives it its own process group "⇒ the
   whole bats tree" — so TERMing an orphan `timeout` looked like it could take out an unrelated land
   started by the same parent. **Measured and false**: a probe spawning a `timeout` and a sibling
   from one shell shows the timeout takes its OWN pgid (distinct from the shell's) and the sibling
   SURVIVES its TERM. Blast radius is pid-only.

2. **"The residual kills are the orphan-tool gap (cause 2)."** The recurring reaped payload is
   `timeout -k 10 900 bash …cloud-return.sh --sweep`, spawned by `autonomy-sweep.sh:907`.
   `cloud-return` is **not** on the whitelist, so cause 2's fix does not exempt it — and killing
   cloud-return cannot TERM a ship-land process anyway. The co-occurrence is one sweep TERMing
   several unrelated things at once, not causation.

3. **"`autonomy-sweep.bats` blowing a 5400 s ladder bound is what blocks the live layer."** The log
   line names `RETRY_TO` unconditionally while `retry_once` has TWO bounds (`FILE_TO`=300 s,
   `RETRY_TO`=5400 s) and rc 124 is identical from either — a MESSAGE bug. A 5400 s bound cannot
   fire in runs whose own totals were 3364/3538 s, which those very cuts recorded. The suite
   measures 77 s / 55-of-55 green. Credit: session 111.

## STILL OPEN — the residual

10 of 14 post-live kills still have a reaper `garbage: TERM` within the preceding 15 s, and the
enrichment over the post-live base rate (27 TERMing sweeps in 7 h ⇒ 15 s shadows cover ~1.6%) is
still ~44x. Yet no land is classified, and the process-group route is refuted. Something about a
sweep coincides with a land death and is not yet named.

The unexplored candidate, stated so the next reader starts there rather than where this one did:
**the sweep is expensive** — two full `ps -Axo` scans over thousands of processes plus classify —
and ship-land's gate bounds its own children with `timeout -k 10 <bound>` (`ship-land.sh:1585`,
`:412`). A load spike would fire a land's OWN bound and surface as SIGTERM 143 through ship-land's
trap, with no external killer at all. The killed lands are consistent with this: they are dominated
by `smoke:"none-unreached"` with `gate_s` ≈ `total_s` (the whole life was the gate), median
`total_s` 99 s. **Test it by recording ELAPSED beside the rc** at each internal bound (memory:
`timeout-rc-collides-with-the-childs-own-rc` — 124 has two authors) and by correlating sweep start
times, not sweep log lines, against gate-step deaths.

## Instrument defects found while investigating (both fixed)

- The garbage arm logged only an aggregate (`N candidates, M TERMed`) on the path that ACTS; the
  per-candidate detail existed only on the dry-run branch. `grep orphan-bash` over 4.4 MB of live
  reaper log returned ZERO lines while the arm was killing every land. Fixed in `a87f32c66`.
- That per-candidate line then truncated argv at 60 chars — and this repo's directory prefix alone
  is 57, so every script logged as `…/claude-infrastructure/scrip` and the line could not answer the
  one question it exists for. Cost a full diagnostic pass. Widened to 120 in `c92c6fe77`.
- `ship-land` reports a **hook-refused** push as `non-fast-forward — a sibling beat you inside the
  window`. Three consecutive attempts on `claude/fire-20260815T093227Z-79031-1` were chased as races
  while the real cause was `pre-push: BLOCKED — 5 commit(s) would be UNATTRIBUTABLE` (author
  `noreply@anthropic.com`, from a `Co-Authored-By: Claude` trailer the commit-msg hook also blocks).
  Filed as backlog `a85dbba865f3`.
