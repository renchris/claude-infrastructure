## 5. Why the class recurs: the structural property

**A pane's end state is re-derived, never recorded.** The session decides its end once, upstream, with the best information it will ever have: "Good to close: yes", `self-close --terminal --allow-dirty`, a custody return. That decision is not written as an authoritative fact that the actors after it must obey. Three things then turn it into a husk:

1. **The husk is the fail-closed default.** The recycle-survivability invariant (08-26) makes every fleet pane end in an interactive shell (`cc-pane-runner:46`, `reso-resume-one:1216`). Closing is therefore a separate opt-in act. A later process with less context performs it, behind a conjunction of fail-closed gates. Any gate that says "no" or "can't tell" sends the pane to the sink, and the sink is a bare shell with a banner. Aug-04 C3 said so ("external close is mandatory"), and so did 09-30 ("nothing closes a pane from inside"). F-a (`74baff1ee`) closes from inside only when the runner's own guard read passes at the moment of death. Pane 289's read was refused-fs, so it fell back to the external path.
2. **At least five components re-derive "meant to end / work at risk / who owns it" independently**, each with its own predicate and its own order of precedence: handoff-fire's self-close gates, the cc-pane-runner guard, cc-pane-close's 4 gates, cc-husk-sweep's verdict_of + COLLECTED + lineage, the watchdog's classifier, and cc-pane-verdict's recompute. Five of them carry their own whole-tree dirty check, and the attribution tool reaches only the teammate path. On pane 289 they disagreed four ways: the watchdog said "ended cleanly, close yes", the closer refused, the verdict said RESUME, and the sweep said COLLECTED. The repo's own lesson names this: "when two tools judge one population, the one that BLOCKS is not automatically the one whose standard is right" (.claude/rules/agent-operating-lessons-situational.md:81).
3. **Each round fixes the incident's spelling, not the invariant.** The plan requires "a bats arm that fails on pre-fix trunk" for each item (pane-end-of-life plan, Wave C). Every round's tests therefore reproduce the last fixture: deaf kitty, teammate suffix, nested claude, background env. None asserts "a session whose terminal intent is recorded ends with its pane closed, in every cwd shape". Each round also *adds* a re-deriving component or gate: cc-pane-close (10-01), cc-pane-verdict (10-22), the drainer job (10-23). That multiplies the ways the components can disagree. Shared checkouts (no worktree, ~/Development/personal) are a cwd shape that no fixture models. There is still no world-sampled alarm on husk count, the very method Aug-04 §5 prescribed after four premature victories.
4. **Moment-of-death facts are frozen.** The guard verdict, the dirty count and the custody state are read once and rendered as permanent ("fixed fact", handoff-fire.sh:1873-1876). The repaint and retry machinery re-reads only the facts that someone remembered to re-read. And when it gives up ("operator owns it"), it drops ownership entirely.

**What follows for the fix:** keep one durable end-of-life decision per session/pane, and make every closer, painter and sweep *evaluate that same record with one shared function*. The record is the session's terminal intent plus the dirt attributed to it (`session_dirty_mine`), never the whole tree. Pair that with an outcome census: kitty bare-shell panes × decisions → an alarm on any pane with no owner. Adding another gate or another painter repeats the pattern.
