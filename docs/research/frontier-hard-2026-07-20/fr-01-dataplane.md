## Unification note for synthesis

F1, F3 and F4 are three faces of one absent primitive: **a lease — an ownership record bound to the worker's attested liveness, carrying the observed prior state, and typed by the resource it holds.** A lease makes the CAS predicate in F1 expressible, makes F3's oracle evidence-based rather than pid-based, and gives F4 the resource handle that serializes trunk-landing. F2 (identity) and F5 (provenance) are orthogonal and must land *first* — an item you cannot uniquely name and whose mutations you cannot attribute cannot hold a lease. Suggested campaign framing: **one work-graph substrate — subject-keyed identity + mandatory provenance + typed leases — replacing five accreted guards.** The incremental-guard path is empirically losing: four guards in eight days, class recurred today.
