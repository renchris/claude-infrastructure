## Mechanisms seen in this shard (my judgment, not the transcripts' own diagnosis)

1. **Rubric switch, not discovery (66, 68, 69).** "Complete / exhaustively done" is computed against the session's frozen DoD. "100.00/100.00" asks against the whole program. The same known, dated residuals get re-rendered as gaps. The operator experiences this as moving goalposts, but nothing new was learned.
2. **Research-to-plan transcription loss (72, 76, also 74's 5→3 drop).** Findings present in the research doc were absent from the plan or the summary. There is no mechanical traceability check (each finding mapped to a plan item or a recorded rejection). A MECE re-render forced by the operator is what caught it each time.
3. **Evidence tier escalates one ask at a time (67, 71, 73).** Tiers ran in sequence: blog or docs, then source read, then code grounding, then execution. Each deeper tier reversed or corrected conclusions from the tier above. All tiers were available from the start; they were spent in increments, one per operator push.
4. **Runnable checks deferred into "plan waves" (73).** The local ASR test on stored recordings and the compile checks were feasible at plan time. Their results changed the design (Parakeet Unified; the race cannot meet the target).
5. **Stale or unchallenged baseline (67; 73 downstream).** A sibling commit changed reality hours before the recommendation. The fork's upstream drift of 223 commits was never in the frame until the operator asked.
6. **Candidate set not enumerated from the artifact itself (73).** Parakeet Unified was already in the fork but was absent from the options list.
7. **Operator-introduced scope (68, 75).** These are genuine new requirements, not research holes.
8. **Pressure to "find gaps" produces items that later prove moot (76).** Gap 1, the "biggest gap", was later DROPPED once measured (`7640e8326`, stock 36/36). Without a materiality threshold set in advance, each ask produces something.
9. **Ephemeral evidence (74).** Research artifacts lived in /tmp until the operator asked for them to be saved.
