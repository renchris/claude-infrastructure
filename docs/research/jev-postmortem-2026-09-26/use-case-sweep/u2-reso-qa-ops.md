## Rejected candidates

- **Flake vs real on land and post-land.** The judgment is `ship-land.sh:696-769` (timeout-only re-run) and `postland-verify.sh:286` (`playwright_failed_only_on_time`). Volume is small: 21 suite-red lands out of 308 in 30 d (`~/.reso/land.log`, v3 rows), and 42 non-green stamps out of 117 since 08-02 (`~/.reso/stamps`). I re-applied the regex signals to the 9 red design-gate logs that still exist. 5 are clear pixel diffs (72–324 screenshot failures, later re-blessed), 2 are timeout-only (the 09-24 operator ruling already handles these), 1 is mixed and 1 has no tally. The signals are structural, and the gate policy is deterministic fail-toward-RED, so a probabilistic judge adds risk and nothing more.
- **`scripts/classify-design-gate-failure.sh` INDETERMINATE (exit 13) residue.** I found 0 classifier outputs in the 50 verify-log dirs, and postland uses its own function. There is no volume to replace.
- **VRT and screenshot verdicts.** The real judgment is on images (intended repaint vs regression, e.g. `ddffdc241` re-bless). Jev is text-only, and the text form is already a regex pixel count.
- **Alarm ↔ runbook match.** It is deterministic: `| Runbook: docs/runbooks/X.md` in AlarmDescription (34 refs to 5 unique runbooks, `git grep`), verified by `audit-runbook-links.sh`.
- **Alarm email and log-line classification.** Alarms go to one SNS topic with one email subscriber. The routing invariants (`.claude/rules/monitoring.md` "Alarm Notification Invariant") are designed so that every email is actionable, and subjects are alarm names. Mail volume could not be measured offline. There is no Sentry: `git grep -i sentry` finds only incidental mentions. `dead-monitoring.ts` is deterministic.
- **Destructive-migration and PR risk.** `lint-migrations.sh` and `deploy-release.sh:124-183` detect DROP/rename on SQL exactly, and the STOP-ASK is intentionally human. A regex beats a judge here.
- **Failure → owning area.** A path→lane map is deterministic (`SKILL.md` Discovery list, `docs/OWNERS.tsv`).
- **curl-gate unparsed "ask".** 741 in 30 d (`~/.reso/curl-audit.jsonl`), but the hook is claude-infrastructure's (`hooks/curl-gate.py`). Its 09-24 variable-resolution fix cut the residue to 28 in ~2.5 days (measured, rows since 09-25). It is a security gate, and the residue includes `.env.local`-sourced credentialed calls.
- **QA finding severity.** Every recent digest's findings are environment blockers (e.g. undici `8.10.0` vs `8.10.2` stale install), not verdicts to judge.
- **Out of scope, pointer for the data/product sweep.** `scripts/checks/fabricated-menu.sh` is a 3-name blocklist that missed paraphrased menu rows for five months (fixed in `451c8eecb`), a customer-facing heuristic with a known miss. The file itself says the correct fix is a diff against the source. `scripts/bottle-gen-validate.ts` is an image LLM judge, so Jev cannot replace it.
