*Axis `cost-gate` of the jcode due-diligence wave `wf_363ad50b-f2b`. Synthesis: [`../jcode-due-diligence-2026-08-11.md`](../jcode-due-diligence-2026-08-11.md).*

## Unknowns

- PRIMARY-SOURCE CONFIRMATION OF THE ANTHROPIC POLICY — the single measurement that would resolve this axis. Both official URLs I tried returned HTTP 404 (anthropic.com/legal/legal-and-compliance and the support.claude.com third-party-integrations article), so the ban rests on six agreeing secondaries plus one verbatim server error string. The resolving read: fetch Anthropic's live Usage Policy / Consumer ToS §3.7 and the Legal & Compliance 'Authentication and credential use' section and confirm the sentence 'Using OAuth tokens obtained through Claude Free, Pro, or Max accounts in any other product, tool, or service … is not permitted'. Note the verdict is FAIL, not UNKNOWN: even a permissive reading leaves the Auto-mode metered fallback and the impersonation-by-design intact.
- WHETHER ANTHROPIC CURRENTLY DETECTS JCODE SPECIFICALLY. jcode has 16,951 stars and its spoof set may be effective today. But evasion efficacy is not permission, and this is unmeasurable from outside without running it — which is the act the gate forbids. Do not read 'many users are not visibly banned' as evidence of safety.
- WHETHER JCODE'S CLAUDE USAGE ACTUALLY LANDS ON THE 5h/7d PLAN WINDOWS IN PRACTICE. Inferred from jcode reading the subscription usage endpoint and from server-side indistinguishability, not observed. Confirming it requires issuing prohibited traffic on a live Max account, so it should stay INFERRED rather than be measured.
- OPENAI'S POSITION ON `originator: codex_cli_rs` SPOOFING BY THIRD-PARTY HARNESSES. Entirely unmeasured. The jcode-on-Codex path is the only gate-passing configuration, and I have not tested whether the Anthropic finding generalises to it — providers.json's codex row was established for the FIRST-PARTY Codex CLI, not for a third-party harness presenting its originator string.
- WHETHER THE BARE `Claude Code-credentials` KEYCHAIN ITEM BELONGS TO A LIVE ACCOUNT. Measured that it exists alongside 11 suffixed items; did not read its payload or map it to a config dir. Relevant only if a trial were authorised — jcode's import would silently target it rather than a routed account.
