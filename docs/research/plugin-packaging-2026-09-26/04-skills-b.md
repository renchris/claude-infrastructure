## Alternatives considered and ruled out

- **Treating repo-wiki as original because it was "re-derived".** Ruled out: `grep` on the Grok-Wiki bundle found four verbatim phrases (see its row). The text is derivative even where the method is not.
- **Treating visual-direction as T2 by dropping the ui.sh sections.** Ruled out: what remains matches Anthropic's first-party `frontend-design` line for line in its key claims (the cream/terracotta tell, 4-6 hex values, "remove one accessory"), so shipping it would be a weaker fork of an installable plugin.
- **kpmg-deck as T2 by relabelling it "any brand via JSON"** (as `SKILL.md`'s description claims). Ruled out: the package, the default brand, 36 KB of brand reference and the examples are all KPMG, and `brand-kit.md` quotes a KPMG publication page by page. Removing that is a product rename, not a decoupling edit.
