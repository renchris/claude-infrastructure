# Self-close: panes that were told to close and didn't (2026-10-01)

Read-only investigation. Trunk = origin/main @ 1343ea87c. All line numbers are `scripts/handoff-fire.sh` on trunk unless another file is named.

**Verdict.** There are two real defects behind the three open panes, and neither is fully fixed on any branch.

1. **The kitty-id stamp gate refuses genuine fired peers.** The `stale` arm refuses without trying the proof the script already has (the fire contract and marker in the session's own first message). That proof runs only when no stamp exists at all. 3 of the 5 stale-stamp refusals this week were genuine peers, pane 47 among them.
2. **A terminal that doesn't answer is read as a missing pane.** The `husk-pane-closers` branch fixes the verdict and adds a short in-call wait. Its durable retry row depends on a library that exists only on another unlanded branch (`fix/husk-fb`), so on its own branch that half does nothing.

The other refusal classes (origin session, dirty tree, open-work ledger, pane identity, spent stamp) are intended and protect something real. Once a close gets past the gates it works: all 151 physical closes logged this week succeeded.

Pane 10 runs under `bin/reso-resume-one`, which falls through to `exec "${SHELL}" -l -i` at `:712`. Pane 32's closing advice, "Typing /exit here closes it", is therefore wrong until F-a lands. `fix/husk-fa` (9596e7fd0) covers `cc-pane-runner` and an opt-in for `cc-close-attrib`. It does **not** cover `bin/reso-resume-one:712`.

## 2. Recommended improvements, ranked

1. **Land the husk family in dependency order: `fix/husk-fb` before `fix/husk-pane-closers`, with `fix/husk-fa`.** Rebase pane-closers onto fb, or land fb first. As it stands, 3c458424b's durable row is a warning-only no-op (`sc_pcq_load`, 3c458424b hunk at `:~3260`). Also, 3 arms × 75 s plus the probe windows runs past the default 120 s Bash tool timeout, and the self-close call is usually run without a timeout. On 09-29 two self-closes were "moved to the background" this way. Either cap the total wait under about 100 s or say in the brief trailer to run it with `timeout: 600000`. Conviction **95%** on the dependency gap, **70%** on the timeout point.
2. **Let brief-contract repair and marker-proven adoption run on `stale`.** At `:10464` widen `[ "$SC_STAMP_STATE" = absent ]` to `absent|stale`, and at `:10370` likewise for `adopt_orphan_stamp`. Move the stale record aside before `mark_fired_peer` overwrites it (for example `<id>.json.superseded-<ts>`). The proof is the same one the `absent` path already trusts: the trailer and an inbound marker in the first user message. An operator session can't produce that. The comment's objection ("two contradictory contracts", `:10368`) doesn't apply, because a stale stamp's cwd provably differs, so it is not this pane's contract. This would have closed 3 of the 5 stale refusals this week, pane 47 included. It also answers question B: pane identity was already proven (`verify_self_pane` passed, and `KITTY_WINDOW_ID=47` matches registry row 47 for sid aee9bc26, pid 96529). Authorization was missing, and the brief proves authorization. Conviction **85%**.
3. **Before trusting `valid` or `unknown`, check whose stamp it is.** Near `:10359`, if the stamp has a `marker` and `fired_marker_is_mine "$marker" "$SC_SID"` fails, treat the state as `stale`, which then flows into recommendation 2. This closes M9: pane 10 would adopt its real `1369.json` instead of writing over a stranger's 09-16 record. Conviction **75%**.
4. **Write closedAt and discharge custody only after the arm is proven, or roll both back on the watcher abort.** Move `record_close_succession` (`:10864`) and the custody discharge to after `await_pane_proof` succeeds (`:10985` on trunk; after the new loop's `break` on the branch). Otherwise undo both in the abort branch. On trunk, an aborted close still tells the originator "returned" and makes the pane read as retired. The spent-retry arm keeps working, because an `absent`/`valid` stamp passes anyway. Conviction **70%**.
5. **Apply F-a's close-from-inside behavior to `bin/reso-resume-one:712`.** On a clean exit with no recycle pending, exit instead of exec'ing a shell, reusing `lib/pane-recycle-pending.sh` from husk-fa. Every post-reboot resume goes through this launcher, and pane 10 is one. Conviction **75%**.
6. **Fix the `--window`/os-window duplicate fire (M11).** At `:14479` (spawn) and `:14444` (`spawn_frontmost`), treat kt_launch's UNKNOWN as rc 13, the same as `it2_split` after 0e403417d. On rc 13, fire-cleanup must not remove the worktree. This caused pane 47's missing stamp and the duplicated W7c work. Conviction **70%**. I didn't trace the cleanup trap's exact line.
7. **For self-close rows, have the drainer wake the session rather than only notify it.** husk-fb's drainer sends a `cc-notify`, and an idle peer only reads mail if its wake path is armed. Either confirm that every fired peer keeps one armed after its DONE ping, or have the drainer use `session-continue.sh`-style wake-up. Conviction **60%**, because I didn't measure wake-path coverage of idle peers.
8. **Fired-peer close turn while the operator is present (M8).** Have the trailer say: answer the operator, then retire after a short grace period. One option is a `self-close --terminal --after 120` that arms the watcher with a delay. This is a UX choice for the operator, so I'd present it rather than build it. Conviction **55%**.
