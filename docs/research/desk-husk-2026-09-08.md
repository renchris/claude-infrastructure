# The desk died at a shell prompt that looked like a clean exit — 2026-09-08

**Session:** `88e2c2b1-ad2b-407a-a889-e7fd15c8076e` · the DESK (role pointer `cc-roles/desk` = pane 330) ·
account store `.claude-tertiary` (launcher `claude3`) · pid 63977 under close-attrib wrapper 63816 ·
tty `ttys015` · cwd `~/Development/claude-infrastructure` (shared checkout).

## Verdict

The session was **killed from outside, while idle, by a SIGTERM that reached its whole process
group** — not a self-close, not a recycle, not `/exit`, not a crash of its own. Nothing of the
desk's own work was lost: in its 16 minutes it wrote no files and made no commits (it notified two
peers and read state; the 22 dirty paths in the shared checkout predate it — `completion-assert`
said so at its last Stop). What was lost was the **desk role**: no desk existed from 22:47Z until it
was resumed, and every page addressed to the desk in that window landed in a dead inbox.

The pane stayed open at a shell prompt showing Claude Code's ordinary `Resume this session with:`
line — byte-identical to a clean exit — because the killer never closed it and nothing else painted
a verdict there. That ambiguity is what this note fixes.

## What the signal was, and what it was not

`sig_reached_wrapper=TERM` in the close record means the **wrapper's own trap ran** — the signal
was delivered to the wrapper (or its process group / tty / argv pattern), not just to the claude
child. A pid-targeted `kill <claude-pid>` leaves that field empty (cc-close-attrib's `_forward`
block explains the distinction). So the sender was group-shaped: `kill -- -<pgid>`, `pkill -t
ttys015`, or a `pkill -f` whose pattern matched both argv lines.

Excluded by evidence:

- **cc-reaper** (the box's most destructive actuator): its garbage arm logs every TERM per pid; the
  nine it sent in that sweep are launcher zsh's of *other* worktrees and three orphan bash's, none in
  the desk's tree; the desk's wrapper was an ancestor of a live claude and therefore in the arm's
  `above[]` protected set; the stuck-wrapper arm needs age ≥ 1800 s (the wrapper was 964 s old) and
  also logs. Only one claude death was recorded fleet-wide in 22:40–23:00Z.
- **cc-teardown** (kill -TERM pid → it2 close): writes a record per act; none after 13:52Z.
- **handoff-fire self-close / --recycle, cc-await-ping --stand-down, cc-pane-headless, gate-cleanup,
  devserver-census, capacity-ramp, offbox-run, postland-verify**: each kills only its own pids/claims
  or a chain it built; none logs an act in the window; every Bash-tool kill verb fleet-wide in
  22:30–22:50Z is in `bash-commands.log` and none targets this tree (the only pattern kill,
  `pkill -f "spec_from_loader('prof'"` at 22:32Z, cannot match it).
- **compressor-sentinel**: SIGSTOP/SIGKILL only, spares `claude*`; no snap-log activity at 22:47Z.
- **desk-invariant / desk-recycle-invariant**: page-only by design law; desk-invariant is not even
  loaded.
- **jetsam**: sends SIGKILL, not SIGTERM; no report near the death.
- **Ctrl-C / it2 close / a pane close**: would have recorded INT or HUP, and the pane survived.

Not excluded: a process outside this repo (the personal `msg`/`agent-browser` tooling was active in
another session at 22:47:36Z), or the operator's own shell. The unified log holds nothing for
`launchd` in that window at info level. **The sender is unattributed**, and this note does not
guess. The one instrument that could name a group-shaped sender is an `SA_SIGINFO` side-car living
in the wrapper's own process group (cc-await-ping already carries the python for it); it costs
~10–15 MB RSS per session on a box that runs a memory compressor sentinel, so it is not added here.

## Why nobody saw it for three hours

Three arms each did the right thing into the void:

1. **The death page** was addressed to `role=desk`. The role resolves to pane 330 — the victim.
   `cc-notify` appended it to a dead inbox and returned 0, which the watchdog logged as DELIVERED.
2. **The supervisor** recorded the page as "no live desk" and folded it into a Notification Center
   digest reading `causes=DEAD` — one word, no session, no command.
3. **The pane** — the only thing the operator looked at — showed the ordinary resume banner over a
   live prompt. `cc-husk-sweep` (built 2026-09-04 for exactly this shape) classifies pane 330
   correctly as `external-sigterm · RESUME`, but nothing runs it unless a human types it, and the
   page that names it went to the dead desk.

And the fourth arm, the **desk-existence invariant**, was disabled at the domain level, so no
replacement desk was ever fired.

## What changed (this branch)

- `hooks/lead-crash-watchdog.sh` — **the pane verdict.** At registration the watchdog now records
  the lead's tty, pane id, config dir and cwd by value. When the lead dies it waits up to 45 s for
  the pane to settle, then — only if the tty still exists and holds nothing but shells — writes a
  verdict block straight to `/dev/ttysNNN` (no terminal API; the launchd-context blindness of
  41d08635a cannot reach a `write(2)`): `⛔ NOT A CLEAN EXIT … KILLED` with the cause and the
  **pinned-launcher** resume line for a CRASH; `✅ CLOSED CLEANLY … safe to close` for a clean exit
  whose pane was left behind (or a warning if its cwd still holds work); `✅ RETIRED … do NOT
  resume` for a desk retirement whose pane close failed. A recycle or resume that relaunched, a pane
  that closed, or a running command on the tty all leave it silent. Kill switch `CC_PANE_VERDICT=0`.
  New entrypoint `--pane-verdict` (used to repaint pane 330 itself, and by the tests).
- `hooks/lead-crash-watchdog.sh` — **the desk-victim escalation.** When the dead session's pane IS
  the desk pointer, the death is also posted to Notification Center naming the restore command,
  and the log says the page sits in a dead inbox instead of "DELIVERED".
- `bin/cc-husk-sweep` — **a live sid is never a husk's identity.** Measured: pane 658 (a bare kitty
  shell in the shared checkout) resolved via the newest-transcript fallback to the sid of the session
  running in pane 646, verdict RESUME — so `--resume --all` would have typed a live session's resume
  line into an unrelated shell. Both fallbacks now exclude sids `cc-sessions` reports live.
- `tests/lead-crash-pane-verdict.bats` (14, incl. a self-locating RED-PROOF control) and one new
  test in `tests/cc-husk-sweep.bats`.

## What this does NOT change

- The sender of the SIGTERM (above).
- `com.claude.desk-invariant` stays disabled at the domain level — that is backlog `107f27fbb00c`'s
  open question ("deliberate mass-bootout or accidental?"), a call about an autonomous
  session-spawner that this note does not make for the operator.
- `cc-husk-sweep` still verdicts RESUME on a dirty cwd even when the dirt predates the session
  (pane 330's 22 dirty paths are older than the desk); the pane verdict prints the count and the
  session's own last close verdict beside it so the operator can weigh it.
