## Headline

**The telemetry is not eating the budget it measures — it costs 0.6–1.2% of fleet throughput — and the reason it is cheap is a harness truncation that is simultaneously destroying the value of its largest component.** Over the 7 days to 2026-08-16 the four accounts burned 98,367 assistant turns / $20,435 list-price-equivalent. Against that, model-visible hook injection costs ~$56/week (0.27%) and Stop-hook forced turns cost ~$63–190/week (0.3–0.9%), for a total of **$119–246/week = 0.6–1.2%**. The premise in the brief — that the SessionStart hook put 43.3 KB (~12 K tokens) into context on every session start in this repo — is **REFUTED**: the harness caps hook `additionalContext` at ~10,000 characters and replaces anything larger with a 2,323-byte `<persisted-output>` stub. The real defect on this axis is the inverse of bloat. `hooks/dod-persist.sh` concatenates two DoD files (a live 15,320 B repo-key store **plus** a frozen 29,922 B legacy store last written 2026-08-09) into a 44,381-byte block that gets truncated to a 2 KB preview of its **oldest, superseded** capture — the current binding `Scope (frozen):` line is structurally unreachable. Measured across 900 recent transcripts: 263 of 516 DoD injections (51%) were truncated, 100% of all `<persisted-output>` stubs in the fleet come from this one hook, and the persisted file was read back by the model exactly once in 110 sessions. Under the operator's policy — where under-utilizing quota is itself a defect and every account currently paces BEHIND — cutting telemetry tokens is not a lever worth pulling; **fixing the truncation is, because it is a quality restoration that happens to also save 606 KB/week of dead stub bytes.**

## Method

**What I ran.**

1. **Injection census (code).** `python3` dump of `~/.claude/settings.json` `hooks` → 14 SessionStart, 6 UserPromptSubmit, 12 PostToolUse, 16 PreToolUse, 12 Stop, 7 SessionEnd, 3 PreCompact entries. Then `grep -E 'additionalContext|systemMessage|decision:"block"'` across all 79 `hooks/*.sh` to classify each by output channel. This repo has **no** `config/hook-chains.d/` beyond two directories (`posttooluse-bash`, `pretooluse-bash`); the wiring is settings.json-resident.
2. **Bytes actually delivered (transcripts).** The measurement unit is the transcript `attachment` record. `hook_success.stdout` carries what the hook *emitted*; `hook_additional_context.content[]` carries what the model *received*. These differ, and the difference is the whole finding. All byte counts below the delivery line are from `hook_additional_context`.
3. **Threshold bracket.** 900 most-recent deduped transcripts across the four account roots, `realpath`-deduped (the `.claude-next{,2,3,4}` symlink trap in the brief). 516 carried a DoD injection; split into inline vs stubbed and took max-inline / min-stubbed.
4. **Block rate.** `~/.claude/autonomy/idl.jsonl` + its 8 gzipped rotations = 1,013,087 records over 109.0 h. Fires deduped into forced-turn *events* by (sid, 10 s cluster), because two blockers firing on the same Stop cost one turn, not two. **I initially measured only the live 5.17 h file and got 1,171/week; widening to the 4.54 d archive gave 305/week — the live file was a burst window.** The wider number is the one reported.
5. **Denominator.** Full streaming scan of all 712 transcripts with mtime inside 7 d (1.29 GB), parsing every `message.usage` and **filtering by each record's own `timestamp`** against a `2026-08-09T10:28:20Z` cutoff, so a long-lived file does not contribute pre-window turns. 0 records lacked a timestamp.
6. **Per-turn cost.** Stratified sample: 25 most-recent + 15 random per account root = 160 files (4.7% of 3,404), 30,105 assistant turns, with a separate last-10%-of-session stratum (2,973 turns) for the late-session figure.
7. **Positive controls.** Every absence assertion is paired: `operator-readout` strings 0/110 *beside* `ACTIVATION QUEUE` 109/110 in the same scan of the same records; "no other hook overflows the cap" is paired with 36/36 DoD stubs found by the same matcher.

**Sample sizes and coverage.** Transcript population 3,404 deduped files / ~7.3 GB. Threshold scan 900 (26%). Injection-byte census 200 of 711 7-day files (28%). Per-turn usage 160 files / 30,105 turns (4.7% of files). Block rate 4.54 d of a 7 d week (65%). The 7-day token denominator is a **full census** of the 7-day window, not a sample.

**What I could not measure, and why.**

- **Quota units.** There is no usage time-series on disk, so tokens cannot be converted to Max-plan weekly-limit percentage. All costs are list-price-equivalents at Opus 5 rates and are a *proxy* for quota, not a measurement of it. Reported as ASSUMED (finding 23). Do not read "0.6–1.2%" as "0.6–1.2% of the weekly allowance."
- **Whether a block's continuation costs one turn or several.** The idl ledger records the block; joining it to the subsequent assistant messages in the transcript requires a sid↔transcript join I did not build. I report a 1–3 message range rather than imputing a constant.
- **The exact harness cap.** Bracketed to (9,200, 10,431]; almost certainly 10,000 characters, but I have no observation at the boundary and will not impute it.
- **Cache warmth at block time.** Inferred from the ≤10 s gap between turn end and Stop hook vs the 5-min TTL, not measured per event.
- **Whether the `PLAN DEFAULTS` / `PLAN GUARD` injections change model behaviour.** Out of axis — I measured their bytes, not their value.

**Prior art extended, not re-derived.** `CONTEXT_ECONOMY_V2.md` (the window denominator is not durably recorded — which is why every figure here is in tokens and dollars, never fill-%), `HOOK_CHAIN_COST.md` (abstaining hooks cost CPU; finding 22 adds that they cost *zero* tokens), `context-economy-100p-2026-08-11.md` (occupancy p95 558 K — consistent with the measured late-turn cache_read p90 of 674 K).

## What would falsify my headline

The headline has three legs. Each fails to a specific, runnable observation:

1. **"Telemetry is 0.6–1.2% of throughput."** Falsified if a forced turn costs materially more than one turn's worth of tokens. My range assumes a block produces 1–3 assistant messages. **Test:** join `idl.jsonl` fired events to the transcript by `sid` + timestamp and sum the `usage` of every assistant message between the block and the next Stop. If the mean is >10 messages, forced turns pass 3% and the headline number is wrong (the conclusion "not eating the budget" survives up to ~10× before it inverts).
2. **"The 43.3 KB never entered context."** Falsified if the harness bills the *pre-truncation* bytes — i.e. if the API request carries the full 44,381 B while the transcript records only the stub. **Test:** find a session whose SessionStart injection crosses the cap and compare its first-turn `cache_creation_input_tokens` against a sibling session in a repo whose DoD is under the cap; a ~10 K-token gap would mean the truncation is display-only and the leak is real. My `input_tokens ≈ 2` measurement (finding 14) does not distinguish these, because both land in the cached prefix.
3. **"The truncation is a quality failure."** Falsified if the model reliably reads the persisted `-additionalContext.txt` back. Measured at 1/110 sessions (0.9%) — but that scan matched on the literal filename appearing in a `tool_use` record. **Test:** widen the matcher to any `Read` whose path contains `tool-results/`, and check whether the scope is instead recovered from the plan file (`docs/plans/*`) — if sessions routinely re-derive the frozen DoD from disk by another route, the injection failing matters much less than I claim.

A fourth, cheaper falsifier for the whole artifact: **the 4.54 d block-rate window is 65% of a week and one calendar slice.** If block rates are bursty at weekly scale (they are demonstrably bursty at hourly scale — the 5.17 h live window read 3.8× the 4.54 d rate), the 305/week figure is unstable. Re-derive from the next two rotation cycles before treating it as a baseline.
