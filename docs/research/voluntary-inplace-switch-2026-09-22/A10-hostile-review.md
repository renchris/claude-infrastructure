# A10 — hostile review: voluntary in-place account switch

**Verdict: the load-bearing premise is false as stated, and the actuator is redundant with one already shipped.** Ranked, most damaging first.

1. **Quota is spent by WORK, not by residence.** An idle session on the expiring account captures nothing; the strand persists unless work arrives afterwards, and if it does, it could have been fired on that account directly (`handoff-fire --account`). The move is a transfer, not a gain: it also strands whatever the source account had left. **Check:** for each past weekly-reset strand event in `logs/account-utilization.jsonl`, count sessions idle at T-6h that then spent >50k tokens before reset. Near-zero ⇒ zero capture.
2. **The move spends the perishable quota on nothing.** Resume re-ingests the whole transcript cache-cold on the target (prompt cache is account-keyed) and starts the target's 5-hour window. Cost scales with fill — worst for exactly the sessions worth moving. **Check:** transplant one large session and diff `input_tokens`/`cache_creation` of the first post-move turn against the last pre-move turn.
3. **Redundant with the router.** R9's `desk_keys`/general ranking already points NEW sessions at the expiring account and fixed 32pp of strand by changing a constant. **Check:** `desk-strand-replay.py` with the shipped floor — how much residual strand is attributable to *existing idle sessions* rather than to routing?
4. **"Healthy and idle" is unobservable from inside.** Idle is indistinguishable from waiting on children: background Bash, named members (`--agent-id`), a workflow wave, `cc-await-ping`, open custody, an armed `/goal` (dies with the process; `/exit`+relaunch is not `--recycle`, which is the only inheriting path), MCP sessions, a half-typed operator message in the TUI. **Check:** census sessions idle ≥10min for live children, open custody rows, armed goal, non-empty input buffer; any non-trivial rate kills the gate.
5. **No trigger, no hysteresis ⇒ churn.** Forced recovery fires on a discrete wall; a continuous ranking signal oscillates as accounts cross. **Check:** count sign flips of "most perishable account" per hour in the utilization series.
6. **Sample-then-act race.** Between the idleness read and the `/exit` keystroke the session can start a turn (a Stop-hook re-prompt, mail drain, operator keystroke) — `/exit` then lands mid-turn on a live pane. **Check:** measure the decision→keystroke latency and grep transcripts for turns starting inside that gap.
7. **Unmeasurable benefit; net loss even when perfect.** Post-hoc you cannot show the quota would not have been spent anyway, and a session whose history spans two accounts breaks every per-account measurement (`cc-quota-price --census`, strand replay). **Check:** require a randomized control arm on eligible events before shipping; compare used% at reset.

**Tried and failed to break:** the transplant's split-brain lock and hop exemption (`lr-transplant.sh:50-210`) already handle repeated voluntary hops correctly, and the tombstone keeps the source unresumable. Mechanics are sound; the *reason to run them* is not.
