# A deny IS the turn boundary a message lacks

_Relocated VERBATIM from `.claude/rules/agent-operating-lessons.md` (always-loaded tier) — the rules file now carries the hook and links here. Nothing was shortened._

[A deny IS the turn boundary a message lacks](.) — 2026-09-09, cc-backlog `b03eb3f28845` closed REFUTED (`docs/research/refused-worker-standdown-2026-09-09.md`): a gate built to stop an ACT was assumed not to stop the ACTOR, and a residual item was filed against it on that assumption — *"worker-claim-gate stops the WRITE, nothing stops the SESSION"*. It was never measured. Over 2,818 transcripts the population it governs tops out at **3** concurrent sessions per worktree (against the filed 15), **0** of 11 refusals ever produced a post-refusal write, and a refused session runs on a median of **2.4 min / 6 tool calls**. The mechanism is the part worth carrying: this repo had already refuted a mailbox stand-down because *"drain needs a turn boundary a session deep in a tool loop never reaches"* — and a `PreToolUse` deny **is** that boundary, since it returns as the `tool_result` of the call the session just made. ⇒ **before building an actuator for the actor, ask whether the gate you already have speaks at a turn boundary** — an enforcement point chosen for its blast radius may carry the message for free, and the "advisory vs enforcing" split does not divide along mailbox-vs-hook, it divides along *does the channel force a turn*. Three companions, each separable: (a) **the obvious remedy was refuted by COST, and the refutation lives in the outlier** — the single long post-refusal run (1,079 min / 126 tools, 20× every other case) is the one session that was RIGHT to ignore the stand-down (a `done-latched` refusal of a pane spawn while it finished landing its own work; it spawned nothing and wrote nothing), so denying its tools would have bricked an 18-hour legitimate land to save a median of six calls; when a residual's harm is concentrated in one case, READ THAT CASE before designing for it. (b) **A `tool_use` record is an ATTEMPT, not an act** — counting Write/Edit tool_use blocks made two duplicate pairs look like both sessions wrote; pairing each to its `tool_result` inverted it (holder 11/11 and 12/12 landed, duplicate 2/2 and 1/1 denied) and is what proved the gate works BY CONTENT. (c) **grep over transcripts for a gate's own message counts its READERS** — 67 hits, 11 real: 41 sessions were in no dispatch worktree so the gate could not have refused them, and 14 had merely `cat`'d the hook source, the measuring session among them; the predicate must be that the tool_result **starts with** the deny. Same family as [pgrep -f sees briefs] and [Census matches itself]. And where a pre/post arm is unreconstructable (the incident worktrees are pruned), positive-control the INSTRUMENT instead: the same sweep reports 32 simultaneous sessions in another project dir, so "max 3" is a property of the population, not a ceiling of the measurement.
