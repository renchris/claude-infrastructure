# A fixture's pid range is a claim about a SHARED, WRAPPING namespace

_Relocated VERBATIM from `.claude/rules/agent-operating-lessons.md` (always-loaded tier) — the rules file carries the hook and links here. Nothing was shortened._

[A fixture's pid range is a claim about a SHARED, WRAPPING namespace](.) — 2026-09-17, `tests/cc-reaper.bats` + `bin/cc-reaper` (landed): a closed-world process-table fixture hardcoded pids `900NN` and justified it in a COMMENT — *"9xxxx pids never exist on the host, so the KILL escalation pass (which does a REAL kill -0 existence check) skips them by construction."* Nothing executes a comment, and pids are a shared namespace that WRAPS: on the day this was measured **pid 90006 was a live macOS CoreRecents daemon**, so `kill -0 90006` succeeded, the escalation logged a KILL for it, and the exact-set assertion failed. Predicted first, then measured — `KILLSET=[90006 <GSPID>]` — which is what separates a diagnosis from a story that merely fits. ⇒ **a hardcoded id from a shared, allocator-managed namespace is not a safe constant** (pid, port, uid, temp name), and this failure is worse than an ordinary flake because the land gate attributes by REACHABILITY: it convicted three unrelated lands of mine while reproducing identically on a pristine trunk with no diff at all. The A/B against pristine is the whole diagnosis; without it the gate's "1 mapped to YOUR diff" reads as a verdict about your work. Two separable halves. **(a) The cure was to CLOSE the world the test already claimed to have, not to re-number 40 references.** The fixture seam already replaced the process table (`CC_REAPER_GARBAGE_PS_A/_PS_B`) and the program's own header says the actuator "covers all three call sites at once — the TERM pass, the KILL escalation, and the watchdog branch". But the escalation asks a SECOND question the actuator never sees — *did this pid survive the TERM?* — and answered it with a bare `kill -0` against the REAL table. A pid listed in the fixture is FICTIONAL and cannot outlive a TERM; a pid absent from it is one the test really spawned and keeps the real check. One helper, placed above its first caller, and production byte-identical when the seam is unset. ⇒ **when a seam already exists, grep for the call sites that MISSED it before inventing a new mechanism.** **(b) Establish whether the collision can actually SIGNAL before writing it up.** Here it could not — `CC_REAPER_GARBAGE_KILL` replaces kill(1) with a collector on that path, so the only syscall that reached the daemon was the harmless `kill -0` — and saying that precisely is the difference between reporting a test-correctness bug and crying hazard.
