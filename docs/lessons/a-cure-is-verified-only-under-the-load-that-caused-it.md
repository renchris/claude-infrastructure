# A cure is verified only under the load that caused it

_Relocated VERBATIM from `.claude/rules/agent-operating-lessons.md` (always-loaded tier) — the rules file now carries the hook and links here. Nothing was shortened._

[A cure is verified only under the load that caused it](.) — 2026-09-10, cc-backlog `ae75073ef319` (fseventsd "pinned at 100% for 13 days"; landed 9f1b31ebb over the cure `3d28f26eb`): **a post-cure reading taken on a quiet box is a fact about the BOX, not about the fix — so a verification needs a positive control proving the causing load was PRESENT when the reading was taken.** fseventsd measured 4.34% mean / 10.2% peak against ~110% during the fault, and FSEvents delivery 8 ms median against 3/3 TIMEOUT at 30,000 ms; either number alone is consistent with "cured" and with "nothing was happening". What makes them a verdict is the control: churn was re-measured at **40.1 files/s — 167% of the ~24/s** the doc had feared, with `postland-verify` mid-run, load 13.89 and 39 live sessions. Same discipline as a red-proof's pre-fix arm, one level up: **name the load, measure it at the moment of the reading, and state it beside the result.** Three companions, each separable. (a) **CAUSE LIVE + EFFECT DEAD is the mirror of [Cause refuted ≠ effect discharged], and it is the more seductive half**: the filed cause here was real, reproducible and still measurable (claude processes are 100% of `fsevent_add_client`, 127-144/h), so a worker who measured only the cause would have CONFIRMED the item and cut the spawn rate — while the effect had been cured two days earlier. An item's payload is its effect in both directions: a live cause is not a live problem. (b) **Price a proposed cause in the units of the observed cost before accepting it** — 0.052 registrations/s at an implausibly generous 10 ms each is 0.05% of a core, three orders of magnitude below the ~110% observed, which refutes the causal story by arithmetic alone and needs no instrument. A mechanism that cannot reach the magnitude is not a candidate, however well it correlates. (c) **A doc's forward-looking PREDICTION rots exactly like a stale justification and must be refuted IN PLACE** — §6's "it becomes the fan-out multiplicand the moment the daemon is healthy again" was written true-and-untestable while the daemon was dead, and became testable and false the moment it was fixed; left unmarked, the next reader inherits an open exposure that does not exist. Mark it refuted beside the original words (never delete — the prediction is the record of what was believed), and where you decline a remedy, record **the criterion and the command that re-measures it** rather than the verdict, since the verdict is the part that perishes.
