# Bisect the band, never divide the deltas

_Relocated VERBATIM from `.claude/rules/agent-operating-lessons.md` (always-loaded tier) — the rules file now carries the hook and links here. Nothing was shortened._

[Bisect the band, never divide the deltas](.) — 2026-09-15, kitty pane title bars (landed 5edaeb53e; body: memory `quantised-step-must-be-bisected-not-divided.md`): **sizing a QUANTISED step by dividing the deltas of a coarse sample measures your sample spacing, not the quantum — and the wrong quantum can close off a whole design space.** I swept `window_padding_width` at top_pad 0/10/17/25, read rows 31/30/29/28, divided, and got "~16pt per cell". The cell is **22.5pt** (45px at font_size 18 × modify_font cell_height 94%). My own four points were perfectly consistent with the true structure — a 30-row BAND spanning total vpad ~3..25, ~23 wide — because a quantised output changes only at band EDGES. A peer bisected for the edge and got it right. ⇒ **to size a quantised step, BISECT FOR THE BAND EDGE**; the tell that you are in this regime is an integer output that stays constant over ranges of the input (rows, columns, pages, buckets, retries). 🚨 **Why it cost more than a wrong number:** the bad quantum made a reachable design point look IMPOSSIBLE, and I wrote that impossibility into a tracked file as a settled three-way trade with "NO FOURTH POINT" *and* filed a class-C decision packet asking the operator to choose among the three. The fourth corner existed the whole time. A measurement error that misstates a figure is cheap; one that closes a design space converts into ARCHITECTURE and into the operator's decision queue before anyone re-checks it — so a claim of the form "there is no other way" earns a re-measurement before it is written down, not after. Second half, independent and equally reusable: **a compound setting may be paying the cost TWICE, and reading one term hides the other.** `window_padding_width 10 5` is top 10 AND bottom 10 = **20pt total**, and rows depend on the TOTAL; I read the top alone and concluded the reservoir would have to be created at the price of a permanent row, when it already existed split across two edges (consolidating to `22.5 5 0 5` off ↔ `0 5 0 5` on measured 30/30/30 against today's 30). ⇒ before concluding a budget is exhausted, enumerate every term feeding the quantity, especially where a setting's shorthand expands to several edges. Third, the correction to my own correction: **a control that does not reach the bug's regime returns a CLEAN NULL, and a null from the wrong regime is indistinguishable from a refutation** — told the resting config costs a row at ~11% of heights, I swept SINGLE-pane at 880..1020 and got today == new at all seven heights, which reads as a refutation; a single pane pays the delta ONCE so the band is ~one pane-height wide and a coarse sample steps over it. Two stacked panes, same sweep: 900px `[19,19]` → `[18,18]`, everything else identical. I had held the PANE COUNT constant while sweeping the very axis I had just been told about, and two coarse sweeps agreeing on a null are not two pieces of evidence when both step over the same band (extends [[control-fixture-must-reach-the-bugs-regime]]).
