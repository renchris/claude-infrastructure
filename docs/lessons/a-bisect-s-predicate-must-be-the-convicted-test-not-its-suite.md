# A bisect's predicate must be the CONVICTED TEST, not its suite

_Relocated VERBATIM from `.claude/rules/agent-operating-lessons.md` (always-loaded tier) — the rules file now carries the hook and links here. Nothing was shortened._

[A bisect's predicate must be the CONVICTED TEST, not its suite](.) — 2026-09-10, cc-backlog `943d0fd3759f` (post-land RED closed as ALREADY-CURED; the fix that came out of it landed as B33-B36 in `tests/postland-verify-bisect-bound.bats`): **a bisect names a commit, but what it actually measures is whatever its bad-predicate ASKS — and when that predicate is a whole SUITE while the conviction is about ONE TEST, a single load-sensitive sibling elects an innocent commit, and every guard layered on top confirms it.** postland convicted `97758a6323ee` for `tests/memory-index-drain.bats::10 the destination append is idempotent`. Direct A/B refutes it: case 10 PASSES at that sha both isolated and whole-file (23/23), first fails four commits later at `5972228d5` — where a new `already-cited` veto returned rc 4 for what is a healthy idempotent re-run — and was cured by `8460f5ac9`, whose own commit body names case 10 and that regression explicitly. What WAS red at `97758a632` under the walk's `load=34.50` was case **19**, a sibling that `18ed810a7` later pinned with `MID_DEADLINE_S` because, in its own words, the assertion "silently becomes 'was the box quiet enough'" (a measured ~1-in-3 flake that had already blocked three ship-land gates). The walk converged on the last commit before that pin. 🚨 **The guards could not catch it because they inherited the same granularity:** `bisect_reach_ok` correctly did NOT veto (the convicted diff genuinely touches the suite — this is NOT the `*.md`-only ratchet class), and `bisect_culprit_confirm_ok`, whose own contract block states the premise it re-measures as *"the failing test fails AT the culprit"*, re-ran the FILE and duly confirmed. **A guard that re-measures a premise at coarser granularity than the premise is stated at will confirm rather than refute** — so read a guard's stated premise against the object it actually executes. AUTO-REVERT then fired at the innocent sha and applied nothing only because the revert CONFLICTED (rc 90): *a FAILED auto-revert on a mis-attributed culprit is the good outcome, not the incident.* ⇒ **narrow the predicate to the convicted test — and when you do, the narrowing itself opens a strictly worse hole that must be closed in the same commit:** `bats -f '<no match>'` prints `1..0` and exits **0**, which `git bisect run` reads as **GOOD**, so a test that was renamed or does not yet exist at a probed tree drives the walk RIGHTWARD onto a later innocent commit — silently, with no step ever failing. Select first (`bats --count -f`), and treat *parses-but-selects-zero* as 125/SKIP; do NOT fold a PARSE FAILURE into that skip (`--count` exits 1 there, emitting `not ok 1 bats-gather-tests` rather than a number), because a commit that breaks the suite's syntax is a genuine regression whose conviction must survive. Generalizes past bats to any CI that bisects, auto-reverts, or quarantines on a FILE-level or JOB-level signal while filing a TEST-level claim. Two companions from the same close: (a) **an exemption keyed on incidental text cannot say what it MEANT to cover** — B18's `$BATS_BIN` census anchored its one exemption on the literal `printf 'TMPDIR=%q`, so adding two emitting lines to the same template matched one by accident and missed the one that mattered; re-key such a census on an explicit marker plus an EXACT tally, and exclude comment lines or the tally moves whenever the prose does. (b) **a stub blind to the axis under test holds it constant** — a bats stub that ignores `-f` cannot express this bug at all, and 31 passing cases in the very suite that owns the bisect's contract were decorative on it (same family as *fixture-identifier-shape-collapses-two-spaces*).
