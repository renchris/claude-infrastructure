Measured 10 such events in the first hours across 11-13 live panes — every one of which the first version would have reported as `REORDERED`. That is not an ordinary false positive: the tool exists specifically to supply the one proof a session must NOT manufacture (a human action), so its failure mode is fabricating the single piece of evidence nobody else can check, in the direction the session is under pressure to produce. ⇒ **for any detector whose job is to witness a PERMUTATION, a stable member set is a PRECONDITION of the verdict word, not a detail** — test `gone or new` first and re-baseline; only a change among members present on BOTH sides may be called a reorder. ⇒ And **control the DIFFER, not just the reader**: the reader here was already controlled against a known-good reorder and that control PASSED, because the bug was entirely downstream of it. Five synthetic branches separate it (true reorder · member closed · member opened · identical · multi-id value that must parse) and the `member closed` case is the only one that fails pre-fix — without it the suite is decorative on the one axis that matters.
