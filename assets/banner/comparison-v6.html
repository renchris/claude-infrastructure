Hero banner — the v6 landscape set

Four art directions on one settled constraint set. 2026-07-29.

The v5 constraints were all satisfied and the result still was not beautiful, so this pass spends everything on the look. What changed the picture most, in the order the renders forced it: a graded sky with a horizon glow (a flat fill reads as paper; air has a gradient), clouds rebuilt four times until they stopped reading as ledges, ziggurats and concrete panels, the opposite builder for ground shapes (slab-plus-bumps makes a city skyline out of a mound), a masked crescent instead of dither, and a ground plane with three values so the bottom of the frame carries weight and the contact shadow registers.

Every variant passes scripts/banner-verify.sh 5/5 — one animation per element, t=0 and t=240 s byte-identical, 12 of 12 sampled frames distinct, dark and light both rendered and different, and a reduced-motion still. Nothing may pass behind the wordmark, and that is now checked at build time rather than by eye: for a scrolling layer an x-position is no exclusion at all, since a cloud at any x travels under the type at some phase, so the generator asserts the Y invariant and fails the build if it is violated.

Where this started

assets/banner/v5a-long-walk.svg

The v5 reference satisfied every constraint and still was not beautiful: a flat sky, dithered clouds that collapse into haze at this size, a moon that reads as a corrupted sprite, and 94 px of dead frame below the horizon. Its light theme is included because it had never once been rendered — --bg light only painted the page behind the image, which a full-bleed background plate hides completely. Inverted clouds, dark against a white sky.

The README edit is deliberately not applied — scripts/banner-apply-header.sh remains unrun, and picking a winner is the operator's call. Regenerate the set with python3 tools/banner/gen.py --out assets/banner; rebuild this page with python3 tools/banner/compare.py.
